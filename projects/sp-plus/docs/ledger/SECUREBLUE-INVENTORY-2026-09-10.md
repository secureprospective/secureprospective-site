**Status: LEADS, not findings.** Per standing doctrine an external reviewer's output is leads
until verified against source. Rows marked VERIFIED were read out of Secureblue's actual config

not an audited fact. Do not adopt a control from a CLAIMED row without reading the implementation.

**One verdict corrected by Claude on triage.** Bee marked *Reject-by-default container policy* as
PORTABLE with the reason "SP+ advisors do not use containers." The reason is wrong and the
mistake matters: `/etc/containers/policy.json` is the file **bootc consults to verify the
operating system image itself** on every update. It has nothing to do with advisors running
containers. That row is the single highest-priority item in `15-DEFENSE-IN-DEPTH-ROADMAP.md`,
not a nice-to-have. See `POSTURE-2026-09-10-alpha4.md` §2.

**The four rows Bee marked REJECTED are correct** under Christopher's day-one rule: the kernel
module blacklist disables Bluetooth, the default service reduction disables CUPS and Avahi and
therefore printing and printer discovery, Flatpak permission lockdown breaks "just about all
Flatpaks by default" in Secureblue's own words, and user-namespace restriction breaks the sandbox
of a native Brave.
