   A bare `from 'jiti'` fails from anywhere.

7. **Thunderbird is NOT in the image.** Do not write rules naming it.
8. **`sp-plus*` / `spplus*` package patterns match nothing.** `/usr/bin/fin` is owned by no
   package, so Fin cannot be uninstalled at all — stronger than a rule.
9. **The C1 "organize" probe proved nothing.** Broken fixture: one item, named FinTest, canary
