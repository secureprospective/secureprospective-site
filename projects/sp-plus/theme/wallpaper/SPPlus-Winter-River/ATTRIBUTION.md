# SP+ Winter River

Source file supplied by Christopher from his own wallpaper collection:
`~/Pictures/backgrounds/wp12970508-winter-artwork-wallpapers.jpg`, 3000x2000
JPEG, dated 2024-08-05.

The `wp########` filename is the naming convention of a wallpaper aggregator
site, so the ORIGINAL AUTHOR AND LICENCE ARE NOT ESTABLISHED. This is recorded
here rather than guessed at. Before SP+ ships publicly, either the upstream
licence must be confirmed or this image must be replaced. It is fine for
internal builds and test ISOs in the meantime.

THIS SOURCE IS 3:2, NOT 16:9, AND THE CROP IS DELIBERATELY OFF-CENTRE. At
3000x2000 it needs 312 lines removed to reach 16:9. A centre crop would take
156 from the top, which clips the crescent moon and its glow in the upper
right. The crop is therefore 3000x1688+0+60: 60 lines off the top, 252 off the
bottom, where the loss is foreground snow and plants. This was checked by
looking at the cropped result, not assumed.

The largest shipped size is that 3000x1688 crop. The source is 3000 pixels
wide, so 3840x2160 would be an upscale and is deliberately not shipped.
