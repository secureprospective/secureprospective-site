# SP+ Moonrise Range

Source file supplied by Christopher from his own wallpaper collection:
`~/Pictures/backgrounds/wp12793013-night-mountain-4k-wallpapers.jpg`, 5000x3000
JPEG, dated 2024-08-05.

The `wp########` filename is the naming convention of a wallpaper aggregator
site, so the ORIGINAL AUTHOR AND LICENCE ARE NOT ESTABLISHED. This is recorded
here rather than guessed at. Before SP+ ships publicly, either the upstream
licence must be confirmed or this image must be replaced. It is fine for
internal builds and test ISOs in the meantime.

THIS SOURCE IS 5:3, NOT 16:9. At 5000x3000 it is 1.6667:1, so it is centre-
cropped to 5000x2812 before scaling, removing 94 pixels from the top and 94
from the bottom. That was checked against the composition rather than applied
blind: the moon sits well inside the upper third and survives the crop, and
what is lost at the bottom is unbroken dark foreground hill.

The largest shipped size is 3840x2160, downscaled from that crop. Although the
source is 5000 pixels wide, cropping to 16:9 leaves 2812 lines of height, so a
7680x4320 entry would be an upscale and is deliberately not shipped.
