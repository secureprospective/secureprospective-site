# Scheduling social posts

Social scheduling happens on the **SecureProspective Social** site, not inside the computer itself. SP+ Welcome checks whether that service is available and opens it in your browser. It does not connect an account, write a post, or schedule anything on your behalf.

## Start from Welcome and check availability

Open **SP+ Welcome** and go to **Your services**. The Social card checks the service before it lets you open it. When the card says it is ready, open it and complete sign-in, account connections, and scheduling on the Social site.

A ready card means the service answered the availability check. It does not mean your own social account is connected or that a post has been published. Check those things on the Social site itself.

If Social is still being set up, wait and try again later. If it is unavailable, use **Retry** after checking your internet connection. Welcome does not change any account while it is checking, so an unavailable result does not mean a connection was damaged.

## Connect only what is available

The Social card shows the platforms the service currently reports. A platform marked live is ready to connect on the Social site. A platform waiting for approval is not ready yet. Do not work around that status by adding a browser extension, sharing a password, or connecting through an unrelated service.

When you connect an account, read the service page carefully and use the account your practice approved. Keep the login and approval steps on the Social site. Welcome is only the safe starting point.

## Review a post before scheduling it

Treat a scheduled post like an email that will leave later. Before you schedule it, check the wording, audience, account, date, time, links, and any attached image. Make sure the draft has the practice approval it needs before you put it on a calendar.

Do not include client information, account details, or private meeting information in a post or an image. If a post mentions a client story, pause and use the practice's approval process before scheduling it.

## A schedule is not a guarantee

Social posts are scheduled, not guaranteed. If the service has an outage, it may not catch up a missed post automatically. Review the schedule after the service returns and decide whether to reschedule, revise, or leave the post alone.

The Social service is also not a compliance archive for SEC Marketing Rule purposes. Keep any required approvals, records, or evidence in the practice-approved place rather than assuming the scheduler is the record.

## Use Fin for preparation, not publication

Fin can help you turn a non-sensitive idea into a short draft or a checklist for reviewing a post. Keep the decision to approve and publish with you or the person your practice authorizes. Do not give Fin client names, account information, passwords, or connected-account details.

Try a prompt such as: **"Fin, turn this non-sensitive topic into a short social-post draft for my review. Do not post it or connect any account."**
