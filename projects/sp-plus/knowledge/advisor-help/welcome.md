# What this computer is

SP+ is the operating system on this work computer. It may look unfamiliar after Windows, but daily work is familiar: browser, documents, files, printing, and calls. You do not need Linux knowledge or commands to use it well.

## What SP+ is designed to protect

When this computer is turned off, the storage holding your work is encrypted. Someone who takes the laptop cannot simply remove the drive and read the files on it. Encryption does not protect a document you send to the wrong person, copy to an unprotected drive, or leave visible on an unlocked screen.

## Why a normal mistake is less likely to become a disaster

SP+ keeps its core operating system separate from your work. It uses a complete system image instead of letting ordinary programs rewrite the core a little at a time. That makes routine changes safer and gives a repair a clear starting point.

Hardware can fail, a website can be down, a file can be deleted, and a printer can still be stubborn. It does mean you should not have to gamble on random downloads or technical instructions to recover.

## Use Fin for the difficult part

**Fin is the assistant built into SP+.** Use it for non-sensitive questions or limited computer checks. It can inspect and change the computer. Ask it to explain before a change, but that does not guarantee every action waits for approval.

A Fin question can go to a connected AI provider. Do not paste a password, recovery key, client name, account number, client document, copied email, or full screenshot into a request.

Describe the outcome or problem in your own words. Start with one of these:

- **"Fin, I am new to SP+. Show me where I should go to [task]."**
- **"Fin, [plain description of the problem]. Check this computer, explain what you find, and do not change anything yet."**
- **"Fin, I need to [print, connect a device, or set up an app]. Explain the next step before changing anything."**

Read [Fin the assistant](asking-the-assistant.md) before relying on it for a difficult issue.

## What stays familiar

SP+ includes a web browser, LibreOffice for documents and spreadsheets, and printer tools. The names and layout may differ from Windows, but the tasks do not. [Finding your way around](getting-around.md) points out the parts you will use most often.

The browser's own password saver is deliberately turned off. Do not assume that a password manager is already installed or signed in. Read [Browser and passwords](brave-and-bitwarden.md) before saving credentials in a second place.

## Updates arrive in the background

SP+ checks and prepares a newer system version in the background. It does not restart the computer in the middle of an appointment. When a notice says a new version is ready, save your work and use a normal **Restart** or **Shut Down** at a suitable break. That completes the prepared update.

You do not need to find an operating-system installer or turn an update setting on. If something behaves differently after restarting, do not keep retrying or download a repair tool. Record what changed, then read [Something broke after an update: going back](../troubleshooting/going-back-after-an-update.md).
