# Video calls, camera and microphone

SP+ includes the browser and audio-video services needed for browser-based calls, but it does not ship a separate Zoom, Teams, or similar call app. Use the meeting service your practice already uses in **Brave**. A successful call still depends on the camera, microphone, headset, and service account available to you.

## Before the call starts

Connect a headset or external camera before opening the meeting page when you can. Then use the meeting service's own preview or test area to check what it sees. Look at the camera picture and say a few words into the microphone before joining, not while a client is waiting.

If your laptop has a physical camera cover, microphone mute switch, or headset mute button, check that first. Those controls override anything a website can do.

## Let the meeting site ask before you allow

A call website may ask to use the camera or microphone. Read the site name in the request and allow it only when it is the meeting service you intended to open. Do not grant camera or microphone access to an unexpected page, browser extension, or pop-up.

If the call page lets you choose devices, select the camera and microphone you are actually using. An unplugged headset or an old external camera can remain selected even after it is gone. If the picture or sound is wrong, leave the meeting page open and ask Fin to check the computer side before changing random settings.

## Keep the call private

Close unrelated client files and messages before joining. If the meeting service offers a choice, share the specific window you mean to show rather than the whole desktop. Check the preview and participant list before discussing client information.

Do not use an unapproved screen-recording, transcription, or browser add-on as a shortcut. Those tools can copy a meeting outside the call. Ask your practice before enabling one, even if another attendee says it is normal.

## When nobody can hear or see you

Start with the physical checks: camera cover open, microphone not muted, headset connected, and the correct device selected on the call page. If you can, leave and rejoin the meeting once after checking them.

Then ask Fin in plain words: **"Fin, nobody can see or hear me on this video call. Check the camera and microphone on this computer first, then tell me what you found."** Fin is set up to check whether the devices exist, are muted or disabled, are selected correctly, and whether the meeting app has permission. It can also offer a short test recording before the next call.

Do not give Fin meeting links, client names, account information, or a recording to diagnose the computer-side problem.

## If the call needs an app or screen sharing

Some practices use a dedicated call app or need a particular screen-sharing setup. SP+ does not include a dedicated call app in the verified starting image. Do not download one from a search result or accept an unexpected browser prompt. Ask Fin: **"Fin, my practice needs [service] for video calls. Explain the approved next step before installing or allowing anything."**
