# Email

Use your practice's own webmail page for day-to-day email. SP+ Welcome can open the Google Workspace or Microsoft 365 sign-in page, but your password goes only into the provider's page. SP+ does not ask for or store your email password.

## Open your work email the safe way

In **SP+ Welcome**, open **Connect this computer to your office** and choose email. Select **Google Workspace** or **Microsoft 365**, then continue to the provider's sign-in page. Check the provider before entering your details.

After it opens the page, Welcome creates a local launcher for that provider in Applications, so you can return to the same webmail page later. The launcher opens the website. It does not connect, read, or manage your mailbox for you.

If your practice uses another email provider, Welcome does not guess which site to open. Ask your practice for the correct webmail address rather than trying a personal account or a search result.

## Why Thunderbird may open instead

**Thunderbird** is installed on SP+, but the verified starting setup has no email account connected to it. It is an option for a practice that deliberately chooses to set up a desktop email app, not a sign that your work mailbox should already be there.

Email-address links can open Thunderbird by default. If that happens while you use webmail, it does not mean anything is broken. Copy the address into a new webmail message, or ask Fin whether Thunderbird is approved before adding an account.

## Check before you attach or send

Email leaves the computer when you send it. Before attaching a file, check the recipient, the file name, and the version. Pause before replying to an unexpected request for client material, a password, or a document. Use a known contact method to confirm it if anything feels out of place.

Do not use a personal email account, a random file-sharing site, or a browser extension as a shortcut for client information. Save attachments in the right place first, then send only through the practice-approved service.

## Let Fin help, but keep sending in your hands

Fin can help you understand an email setup problem or prepare a draft, but you review and send every message yourself. Do not give Fin your email password or paste client names, account numbers, balances, documents, or message contents into a request.

Useful prompts include:

- **"Fin, my Google Workspace sign-in page will not open. Check the computer-side problem before I change anything."**
- **"Fin, help me make a short email template about [non-sensitive topic]. Do not send it."**
- **"Fin, Thunderbird opened when I clicked an email address. Explain the approved next step without setting up an account yet."**

## If you cannot sign in

Start again from the known provider page. If the password is rejected, use the provider's recovery process or contact the person who manages practice email. Do not send a password to Fin or type it into a page you reached through an unexpected pop-up or link.

If the provider page itself will not load, ask Fin before installing anything or changing browser settings. A sign-in problem is not a reason to download a random mail program.
